Admission Process

1. Apply Online

Fill out the application form on our portal with your personal and academic details.

2. Entrance Exam

Appear for WBJEE / JEE Mains for Engineering or relevant entrance tests for other courses.

3. Counseling

Participate in the counseling process and select Techno Main Salt Lake as your preferred institute.

Eligibility Criteria

  • B.Tech: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Valid rank in WBJEE/JEE Mains.
  • BCA: 10+2 with Mathematics/Computer Application as a subject.
  • MBA: Graduation in any discipline. Valid score in MAT/CAT/JEMAT.
  • M.Tech: B.Tech/B.E. in relevant discipline. GATE qualified preferred.
