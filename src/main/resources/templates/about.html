Who We Are

Techno Main Salt Lake is a premier institute of engineering and management in Kolkata, West Bengal. We are committed to providing world-class education and fostering a spirit of innovation and research among our students.


Our campus is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, a vast library, and modern classrooms to ensure a holistic learning experience. We believe in nurturing not just academic excellence but also the overall personality of our students.

Our Vision

To be a widely recognized center of excellence in technical education and research.

Our Mission

To produce industry-ready professionals with ethical values and a commitment to society.

Our History

Establishing benchmarks in academic excellence for over two decades in West Bengal.
